# Mana — Codex & Claude usage in your Mac menu bar

[Mana](https://silent-spell.com) is a 3.7 MB macOS menu bar app that shows how
much of your AI coding quota is left — Codex, Claude Code, Gemini, Antigravity,
Cursor and Grok — as rings you can read at a glance.

<img src="https://silent-spell.com/plugins-menu.png" alt="Mana menu showing Codex, Claude, Antigravity and Grok quotas with reset countdowns" width="420">

**[Download Mana for Mac](https://downloads.silent-spell.com/mana.dmg)** · macOS 14+ · free for one ring

## Why Mana

- **Lightweight.** A 3.7 MB download.
- **No pop-ups.** Built-in providers work without Keychain approval, an extra
  login or Full Disk Access — Mana reads the sessions your AI tools already keep
  on your Mac.
- **Read it without clicking.** Each provider is a ring in the menu bar showing
  up to two remaining quotas, such as Codex's 5-hour and weekly limits.
- **Checking usage costs no usage.** Mana reads usage data only; it never sends
  prompts or spends tokens.
- **Any quota, via plugins.** DeepSeek, Kimi, OpenRouter or your company's
  internal LLM budget — your AI coding agent can write the plugin for you.
- **Verifiable network.** A deny-by-default allowlist keeps traffic to each
  vendor's official API. See the [transparency report](https://silent-spell.com/transparency).

## What it shows

- **Codex:** 5-hour and weekly limits, reset times, Extra Credits, Reset Bank,
  and team workspace monthly limits — [Codex usage in the menu bar](https://silent-spell.com/codex-usage-tracker)
- **Claude Code:** session and weekly limits (including per-model weekly),
  extra usage, Limit Reset count — [Claude usage tracker for Mac](https://silent-spell.com/claude-quota-tracker)
- **Gemini, Antigravity, Cursor, Grok:** remaining quota and reset times —
  [Antigravity quota monitor](https://silent-spell.com/antigravity-quota-monitor)
- **Anything else:** [custom rings with plugins](https://silent-spell.com/plugins)
